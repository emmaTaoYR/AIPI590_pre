Competitive landscape

Different products solve different parts of the return journey.

The market is not empty — but the experience is still fragmented.

REMIND

Return Before

Tracks return deadlines and reminds users before they expire.

DETECT

HawkReturn

Connects to email, detects purchases, and flags refunds at risk.

EXECUTE

ReturnQueen

Offers doorstep pickup and printer-free return execution.

Reminder. Detection. Pickup. Useful pieces — but still separate.
Briefly show that the market already validates parts of the problem. Return Before handles reminders, HawkReturn handles email detection and refund-risk alerts, and ReturnQueen helps with physical execution. The point is not that the market is empty — it is that the workflow remains fragmented.
